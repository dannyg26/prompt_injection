The former cross-source proposal is superseded; its original copy is preserved in the local artifact archive. This revision follows [verified related work](RELATED_WORK.md), not new positive experimental evidence. CAPTURE and WAInjectBench already study adaptation; The published EvoShield experiments do not report this exact comparison; see EVOSHIELD_RESOLUTION.md. Global novelty remains unestablished.

The authoritative proposed design is the [two-page experiment specification](EXPERIMENT_SPEC.md). It is a controlled replication/extension, not a novel-method claim or completed preregistration. The existing small-data runs are [repaired exploratory checks](DATA_REPAIR.md).

Before confirmatory work: resolve the literature overlap, component licenses/provenance, compute availability and independent sample-size gate. If those fail within eight weeks, narrow to a reproducible exploratory replication or review a different question. A defensible negative result is acceptable; an underpowered interval is inconclusive, not evidence of no effect.
