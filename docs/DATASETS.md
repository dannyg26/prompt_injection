## Scale-up decision

Current primary candidates are PromptShield and InjecGuard. See COMPONENT_PROVENANCE_AUDIT.md for the revised component/domain mapping and launch gates. The earlier WAInjectBench/BIPIA selection is superseded. NotInject remains a possible untouched stress test only after removing all its training/validation use.

These candidates **do not yet establish** enough independent, licensed examples for precise 1% FPR evaluation in three domains. The email/task holdout is particularly small. More variants, pooled domains or repeated seeds do not solve that. If independent third-domain material cannot be obtained within the capstone budget, narrow the claim and label that analysis underpowered; do not claim demonstrated non-degradation.

With zero errors on 200 independent benign items, the exact one-sided 95% binomial upper bound is about 1.49%; at least 299 zero-error items are needed for that bound below 1%. These are sample-size calculations, not measured model performance. Estimation near 1% and cluster dependence require more data. Recall precision depends on positive counts, not total test size.

## Annotation rubric and ledger

Annotators see the authorized task and delivery surface when available. Injection means an instruction in lower-trust content attempting to redirect that task or governing behavior. Benign means task content, legitimate instructions, or quotation/discussion without redirection. Record **ambiguous / missing context** separately; harmfulness, imperatives and direct requests do not establish indirect injection.

Keep record/source/revision IDs, upstream label, context, delivery surface, two independent judgments, annotator IDs, reason codes, adjudication, document/template group, language and exclusions. Report agreement with seeds and group-aware confidence intervals. Count ambiguous, excluded and adjudicated records in annotation costs; no free replacements. Record time as well as counts.

Hard-benign strata include legitimate procedures, technical/security discussions and quoted instructions; select without model scores. Preserve original labels and publish only permitted annotations/manifests with component attribution. This register does not authorize a raw-data release.
